### Added
- **The fleet alerts are loaded by every install, with a runbook.** `OpenIDXAgentTunnelDown` and `OpenIDXTunnelDialFailures` lived only in `deployments/monitoring/alerts.yml`, a file neither the Compose stacks nor the Helm chart load. They are now in `deployments/docker/prometheus/alert_rules.yml` and in the chart's PrometheusRule, each with a `runbook_url` pointing at the troubleshooting section of `docs/AGENT-TUNNEL.md`, and `promtool check rules` runs on the Compose and monitoring files in CI beside the chart's.
- **Fleet metrics.** `openidx_agents_by_state{state="active|pending|suspended|revoked"}` counts the enrolled agents per status and `openidx_tunnel_policy_gaps{gap="full_tunnel|block_direct"}` the active devices whose last tunnel status reports that part of the tunnel policy as not enforced; both are install-wide and read at most every 30 seconds, like `openidx_tunnel_agents_down`.

### Changed
- **A device with policy gaps is not "applied".** `tunnel_policy_state` in `GET /api/v1/access/agents` is `applied_with_gaps` for a device that runs the current policy version but reports `policy_gaps`; `applied` now means the current version with no gap, `pending` is unchanged. `GET /api/v1/access/network-policy` counts those devices in a new `devices.applied_with_gaps` beside `applied`, `pending` and `unreported`. Agent Fleet shows **Policy applied, with gaps** for them and the Network policy card counts them apart.
