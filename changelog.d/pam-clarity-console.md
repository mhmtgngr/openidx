### Added
- **My Privileged Access page (`/my-privileged`).** Your remote sessions, session requests and
  credential checkouts on one page, each entry with plain sentences saying what applies
  (recorded, watermarked, moderated, approval, dual control, exclusive, break-glass, reveal,
  zero-trust network), and an empty state that explains what appears there. My Apps & Network
  keeps a summary of up to three with a *View all* link.
- **Notice before a monitored session.** Connecting to an entry that records, is moderated or
  watermarks first shows what is recorded, who can see it and how long it is kept (KVKK). The
  acknowledgement is remembered per entry for 30 days and asked again when the rules change.
- Refused connects and reveals say what to do next (exclusive holder, second approver,
  moderator, device trust, approval, external-user codes, zero-trust reach).
- PAM connections shows rule chips per entry; the reveal dialog says what is audited and how
  long the value stays visible. Links to the *Privileged access* guide.
