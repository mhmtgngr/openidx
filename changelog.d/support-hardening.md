### Added
- **A sponsor is told when their vendor's support session goes live.** The moment a vendor technician's remote-support session becomes active (both the technician and the device connected), the technician's sponsor and the engagement's sponsor, once each, get a **Vendor users you sponsor** notification naming the technician, the device and the engagement, with a link to Access requests → **Vendor support sessions**, where they can watch or end it. Each sponsor told is audited as `remote_support.sponsor_notified` (a failed write as its failure); a sponsor who switched the notification type off is not told, as on the PAM side. It is best-effort and off the relay's path: the session runs whatever the notification does.
- **Vendor session recordings play in the console.** Remote Support → a session with a recording → **Play recording** plays it in the page. A vendor session's recording, which the broker stores as IVF, is served as WebM: the VP8 frames are remuxed, not re-encoded (a pure-Go muxer, no new dependency), with a duration, cues for seeking and an exact Content-Length, timed from the first frame. The stored IVF stays the original: `GET /api/v1/access/remote-support/sessions/:id/recording?format=ivf` serves it byte for byte, and so does a recording the remux cannot read. Download now saves the same playable WebM.

### Changed
- **Watching a vendor's support session asks for a fresh second factor**, as watching a PAM session does. The watch socket (`GET /api/v1/access/remote-support/sponsored/sessions/:id/watch`) is a WebSocket upgrade and bypassed the freshness gate; it now opens only with `?ticket=` from `POST .../sponsored/sessions/:id/watch-ticket` (fresh second factor, action `remote_support.sponsor_watch`), a ticket that opens one socket, within 60 seconds, for that session and that sponsor. A missing, spent, expired or someone else's ticket is a 403 `watch_ticket_required`. The console asks for a ticket before it opens the viewer, and a fresh one before each reconnect. Tickets live in the broker's process, which is single-replica already.
